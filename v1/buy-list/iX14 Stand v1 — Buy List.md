# iX14 Stand v1 — Buy List

### Use from your existing insert kit

- [ ] **2 × M3 heat-set inserts**, approximately **5 mm OD × 5 mm long** — your **M3 × 5 × 5** bin should be the one.
- [ ] **1 × M4 heat-set insert**, approximately **6 mm OD × 4–6 mm long** — use either your **M4 × 4 × 6** or **M4 × 6 × 6** size.

### Buy

- [ ] **1 × M4 × 0.7 spring-loaded ball plunger**
  - Light-load version
  - Approximately **8–9 mm overall threaded-body length**
  - Approximately **2.5 mm ball**
  - Target spring force roughly **2–5 N**
- [ ] **1 × M4 × 60 mm socket-head cap screw**
- [ ] **1 × M4 nyloc nut**
- [ ] **2 × M4 flat washers**, ordinary ~9 mm OD
- [ ] **2 × M3 × 12 mm socket-head or button-head screws**

### Search phrase for the important weird part

**“M4 x 0.7 spring loaded ball plunger 2.5mm ball light load”**

The dimensional target I designed around is very close to an **M4 × 8 mm miniature light-load ball plunger with a 2.5 mm ball**.
