# Assembly Instructions — iX14 Stand V2

[Download the printable three-stage assembly guide (PDF)](ix14-v2-assembly-guide.pdf)

These instructions apply to the internal-handle V2 assembly. **Left and right are always described while looking at the front of the radio.**

> **Hardware status:** the printed mechanism, inserts, pivots, stand interface and full assembly fit have been physically validated. Final spring-loaded ball-plunger behavior remains pending arrival of the production M4 × 8 hardware.

## Insert locations

Install all heat-set inserts before mounting the cases to the radio.

- **4 × M3 inserts:** two reinforced cap-screw pockets in each case body
- **2 × M3 inserts:** one square stand-mounting block on each pivot stub
- **1 × M4 insert:** spring-plunger bore in the right detent case body

Use the alignment feature supplied with your heat-set tool, such as an insert-centering nub, whenever one is available. If your heat-set setup does not already provide an alignment solution, an appropriate screw may be threaded loosely into the insert to help keep it straight. Press the insert into its reinforced collar and allow the part to cool completely before applying load.

## 1. Assemble the right detent pivot

1. Place the replaceable detent ring on the support face of the right detent pivot stub.
2. Align the asymmetric square key and recess. The ring should seat fully without force.
3. Orient the detent pockets toward the right case body and its ball-plunger bore.
4. Slide the right pivot stub over the case body's 7 mm pivot sleeve.
5. Install the M4 pivot bolt through the case body, sleeve and stub.
6. Fit the M4 washer on the Nyloc side, then install the M4 Nyloc nut.
7. Tighten until the hardware is firmly seated. Confirm that the sleeve shoulder prevents the stub from being pinched and that the stub still rotates freely.

## 2. Assemble the left plain pivot

1. Slide the left plain pivot stub over the left case body's pivot sleeve.
2. Install the M4 pivot bolt through the case body, sleeve and stub.
3. Fit the M4 washer on the Nyloc side, then install the M4 Nyloc nut.
4. Tighten until firmly seated and confirm smooth, free rotation.

## 3. Mount the case assemblies to the handle

![Stage 2: attach the case assemblies to the radio handle](images/assembly/step-2.png)

1. Place the completed left and right assemblies around their corresponding iX14 handle rails.
2. Match the cap marked `L` to the left body and the cap marked `R` to the right body.
3. Install two M3 × 12 screws through each cap.
4. Tighten the four cap screws only enough to hold the assemblies in place. Leave enough movement for the stand to square and align both sides.

Washers are not required under the case-cap screws.

## 4. Attach the stand

![Stage 3: attach, square and tighten the stand](images/assembly/step-3.png)

1. Align the stand's two rectangular sockets with the square mounting blocks on the pivot stubs.
2. Seat both blocks completely in the stand sockets.
3. Install one M3 × 8 screw and M3 washer through each stand hole and into the insert in the corresponding stub block.
4. Leave both stand screws slightly loose during alignment.

The square blocks carry the stand's shear and twisting loads; the M3 screws retain and clamp the keyed connection.

## 5. Square and tighten the assembly

1. Push both handle-mounted case assemblies as far toward the radio body as they will naturally seat.
2. Use the attached stand to bring both pivot assemblies square and parallel.
3. Tighten the four M3 × 12 cap screws evenly, alternating between screws so each cap clamps uniformly.
4. Tighten the two M3 × 8 stand screws and washers.
5. Fold and deploy the stand through its complete travel to confirm that neither side binds or shifts.

Do not overtighten the cap screws against the radio handle.

## 6. Install and adjust the ball plunger

1. Thread the M4 × 8 spring-loaded ball plunger into the M4 insert in the right detent case body.
2. Rotate the stand slowly while advancing the plunger until the ball begins engaging the detent pockets.
3. Adjust in small increments until each position clicks positively without making the pivot difficult to rotate.
4. Cycle the stand repeatedly through its full range and confirm that the replaceable ring remains fully seated.

Do not use permanent threadlocker. Complete the initial adjustment and wear-in testing before deciding whether any removable retention method is necessary.

## Final inspection

- Both case assemblies are fully seated toward the radio body
- Left and right caps are correctly matched
- All four cap screws are evenly tightened
- Both pivot bolts are secure with washers and Nyloc nuts
- Both pivots rotate freely without axial looseness
- Detent ring key is fully seated and pockets face the plunger
- Stand blocks are fully seated in both sockets
- Both stand screws have washers and are secure
- Stand deploys smoothly and supports the radio at the intended angle
- Stand folds without contacting the radio body, switches or controls
- Handle opening remains clear for normal carrying

## Safety and maintenance

- This is a desk/simulator stand, not a replacement carrying handle.
- Inspect the printed parts, inserts and fasteners periodically for cracks, loosening or wear.
- Replace the detent ring if its pockets become rounded or inconsistent.
- Recheck cap and stand screws after the first several use cycles.
- Never force the stand beyond its designed range of motion.
- Use the stand at your own risk and verify stability before placing the radio on any elevated, uneven or windy surface.
