# Spektrum iX14 Stand — V2 Recommended Design

V2 is the current and recommended stand design. Its two compact assemblies mount around the individual handle rails, preserving more usable handle space while providing independent retained pivots and a replaceable keyed detent ring.

## Product Images

The [V2 product-images directory](documentation/product-images/) contains photographs of the actual printed and assembled design:

- a direct V1-versus-V2 extension comparison (`00-v1-vs-v2-range.jpg`);
- separate printed components;
- completed assembly;
- folded position;
- normal and maximum extended positions;
- side views showing the increased range of motion;

These photographs document the physical build and its usable positions; they are not CAD renders or assembly diagrams.
