# Adjustable Stand for the Spektrum iX14

A folding, indexed-position desk stand designed specifically for the square handle on the Spektrum iX14 transmitter.

The mount clamps around the factory handle without modifying the radio. A spring-loaded ball plunger engages the detent disk to provide repeatable stand positions, while the stand folds against the back of the transmitter for storage.

## Features

- Designed for the Spektrum iX14 square handle
- Folding stand with indexed detent positions
- Spring-loaded ball-plunger mechanism
- Heat-set inserts for durable assembly
- Separate clamp, ear, detent, and stand components
- Chamfered stand edges that can be colored with Bambu Studio's paint-bucket tool
- Individual STL files plus ready-to-slice Bambu Studio projects
- No permanent modification to the transmitter

## Recommended Material

PETG is recommended for the final radio-mount components because the clamp and ears remain under mechanical load. PLA is suitable for prototypes and fitment tests.

The stand may also be printed in PETG for a consistent final assembly. Dry PETG before printing and use a filament profile appropriate for the exact brand and product line.

Final tested PETG settings will be documented here and embedded in the supplied 3MF projects after the production print is complete.

## Printing Notes

- Nozzle: 0.4 mm
- Layer height: 0.20 mm
- Build plate: Textured PEI
- Print the parts in the orientations provided by the 3MF projects.
- The radio-mount project uses build-plate-only tree supports where needed.
- The stand does not require supports in its supplied orientation.
- Do not scale any part.
- Confirm that the painted chamfer appears correctly in Preview before printing the stand.

## Assembly Overview

1. Install the required M3 and M4 heat-set inserts into the printed parts.
2. Fit the clamp body and cap around the iX14 handle. Tighten the clamp screws evenly; do not overtighten against the radio.
3. Attach the left ear and right detent ear to the clamp assembly.
4. Seat the detent disk in the keyed recess at the top of the stand.
5. Position the stand between the ears and install the M4 pivot bolt, washers, and nyloc nut.
6. Install the spring-loaded ball plunger in the right detent ear.
7. Adjust the pivot tension and ball-plunger depth until the stand moves smoothly and engages each detent positively.
8. Verify the complete range of motion before placing the radio on the stand.

Detailed assembly photographs—including insert locations and ball-plunger installation—will be added with the final production build.

## Important

- This is a desk/simulator stand, not a replacement carrying handle.
- Check the printed parts and hardware periodically for cracking, loosening, or wear.
- Do not force the stand past its designed range of motion.
